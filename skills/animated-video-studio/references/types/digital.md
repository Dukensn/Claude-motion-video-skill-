# Stick vs. the digital world (menu 6)

A coloured stick figure lives inside software and fights it: a spreadsheet, a code editor, a phone's home screen,
a settings menu, a calculator, a maps app, a photo editor, a video game level, a chat, an email inbox, a printer queue.
Wordless action-comedy, 1–4 minutes; the "user" (a cursor, a hand) is the god of this world.

- **Pick a world with rules** and play with them: cells can be kicked, an error is a villain, Ctrl+Z rewinds time,
  copy-paste makes clones, the delete key is a weapon, autocorrect changes the enemy's name.
- **Escalate** world by world (one app → the desktop → the whole OS) or one rule at a time.
- **The cursor** is an ally, an enemy or a twist (it copy-pastes the hero, drags him to the trash).
- **Readable action**: one hero colour, the world in neutral greys; every hit gets an impact + a sound.
- Invent the software's look (generic UI), never real brand logos or a real game's exact levels.

Dice: `python3 SKILL/scripts/ideas.py digital`.
