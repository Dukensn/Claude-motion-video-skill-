# Moral short film (bean engine + temptation, menu 9)

2–5 minutes, wordless except captions; a habit or a choice the viewer recognises (phone at 3 AM, pride, envy,
procrastination, lust vs love, comparison, fear of failing).

**Structure**
1. Cold open card: a line that sets the rule, a second line that twists it ("He had seen a thousand faces. / He had never held one hand.").
2. The habit, shown: same framing, time passing, a **counter** that keeps score (SCROLLED 12,408 / CALLED MOM 0).
3. The temptation appears: a personified emotion (a dark shape with a neon edge, a grin) that whispers in handwriting.
4. The cost, shown not told: a split screen, a missed moment, someone waiting.
5. The choice: the shadow weakens or wins. The counter changes (0 → 1).
6. Closing line + title card ("Five minutes for the feed. / Five minutes for her.").

- Name the temptation in the title (LUST VS LOVE, PRIDE, FIVE MORE MINUTES) — and never preach in captions; let images judge.
- Colour: the world desaturated, the temptation's neon is the only saturated colour, love/real life is warm (amber).
- Pace: 3–4 s shots, fades through black between scenes, rain/clock/phone sounds.
- Endings can be happy, bitter or open — vary them across a student's videos.

Dice: `python3 SKILL/scripts/ideas.py moral`.
