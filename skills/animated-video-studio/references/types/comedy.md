# Comedy skit (cartoon style, menu 8)

Short, visual, fast. 20–60 s for a Short, 2–4 min for a YouTube episode made of 3–5 linked gags.

**Shape of a gag** — set-up (what someone wants, shown in 1–2 s) → the threat or obstacle → the turn-away / the plan →
it goes wrong → it goes *more* wrong (escalation) → the button (one last tiny joke after the big one).

- **Rule of three**: two normal beats, the third breaks the pattern.
- **Reaction shots** are half the comedy: cut to the face after every disaster (big eyes, sweat, dizzy stars).
- **Timing**: hold the beat before the disaster (0.5 s of stillness), then hit fast. Sound effect on every hit.
- **Words**: speech bubbles of 2–5 words, or none. The funniest line is usually the shortest.
- **Characters**: exaggerated silhouettes (a wide baker, a tiny boss, a tall nervous robber), one prop each.
  Animals and objects make great antagonists (a pigeon, a vending machine, a GPS).
- **Tone mix**: you may land on a tender or absurd final beat — it makes people share it.
- Avoid copying existing cartoon characters (no famous villains, no game mascots). Invent them.
