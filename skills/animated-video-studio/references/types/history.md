# History in sketch style (menu 4)

Narrated, 1–12 minutes (or a 60 s Short): one real person, event or era, told with ink drawings on parchment,
maps, dates and a light sense of humour.

**Structure**
1. Hook: the most surprising fact, or a "profile card" (NAME — ✓ General ✓ Strategist ✓ Absolute legend).
2. Where and when: a map, the region hatched, a date stamp.
3. The rise / the problem: 3–5 beats with dates, each with one strong image (an army, a ship, a document).
4. The turn: the betrayal, the mistake, the discovery.
5. The ending + what it changed today, then a last image.

- **Facts must be true**; hedge what historians debate ("around 1743", "he reportedly said"). Check with web search.
- Humour is in the drawings and captions (profile cards, stamps, a sheep in the battle), never mocking victims.
- Costumes from the right period (bicornes, laurels, kabuto, nemes); no fantasy armour on real armies.
- Great for the course's audience: Haitian, Caribbean and African history (Toussaint Louverture, Dessalines,
  Henri Christophe, Queen Nzinga, Mansa Musa, the Taíno…), and world history.
