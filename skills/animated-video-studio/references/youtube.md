# YouTube text

Write `films/<slug>/youtube.md` in the on-screen language:

```
# Title
TITLE. | An Animated Short Film          (≤ 60 characters; or a curiosity line: "He wrote 214 messages. He sent none.")

# Description
Two or three sentences that set up the story without spoiling the ending, ending with a question to the viewer
("Who is the person you keep meaning to call?").

Chapters
0:00 <cold open line>
0:45 <act name>
…                                        (5–7 chapters matching the acts; first at 0:00)

Music and animation made for this channel.
#animation #shortfilm #<theme> #emotional

# Tags
animated short film, emotional animation, <theme> story, <object>, short film about <relationship>, …  (10–15)
```

Upload settings to suggest: not made for kids (unless it truly is), category Film & Animation, language set, thumbnail uploaded custom, end screen pointing to the previous film. Publishing at a steady rhythm (e.g. one per day) beats bursts.

Examples: `SKILL/examples/*_youtube.md`.
