# Intake

Ask these in **one** AskUserQuestion call (max 4 questions, 2–4 options each; the user can always type "Other"). Put the recommended option first.

1. **Style** (header "Style")
   - *Bean line-art (Recommended)* — cream round-headed characters drawn in boiling sketch lines on dark rooms, one accent colour, an emotion personified as a glowing figure. Examples: UNSENT, AGAIN, LITTLE BEAN.
   - *Silhouettes* — black figures with white eyes and coloured sneakers on flat, soft-gradient sets; italic serif title cards. Example: SOMEDAY.
2. **Story** (header "Story")
   - *You choose (Recommended)* — you invent an original story and show the beat sheet first.
   - *Family* (parent/child, regret, time running out) · *Love* (couple, distance, memory) · *Self* (habits, fear, "someday").
   - If they type their own idea, build from it.
3. **On-screen language** (header "Language") — *English (Recommended)* · *French* · *Spanish* · Other. All captions, cards, the thumbnail title and the YouTube text use it.
4. **Length** (header "Length") — *About 4 minutes (Recommended)* · *About 2 minutes* · *About 60 seconds (Shorts, 1080×1920 — see below)*.

Defaults when not asked: accent colour chosen by you per story; no channel name on screen; music = piano + strings (bean) or nylon guitar + music box (silhouette).

If they choose a vertical Short: change `W/H` to 1080×1920 in `core.js` and every `1920`/`1080` in `index.html`, keep captions inside the middle 80 %, and expect to recompose shots — tell them it is a bigger change.

After the answers, restate the brief in two lines and move on to the story. Don't ask further questions until the beat-sheet checkpoint.
